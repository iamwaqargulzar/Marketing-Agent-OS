# Outbound operations

Use for sending readiness, LinkedIn steps, multichannel cadence, and reply handling; copy-only tasks can stay in the entrypoint.

## Readiness and scope

- Identify the permitted population, jurisdictions, actual sender identity, licensed contact source, approved claims, channel permissions, and daily caps. Contact verification does not establish consent or legal eligibility.
- Check current mailbox-provider authentication, alignment, unsubscribe, and complaint requirements. Verify the observed configuration and delivery evidence; do not copy a vendor's volume or warmup schedule as a platform guarantee.
- Separate optional reputation isolation from evasion: do not rotate identities/domains to bypass restrictions, impersonate people, or manufacture warmup engagement.
- Set tracking from the measurement need and privacy constraints. Opens are noisy; replies, qualified meetings, and confirmed outcomes need their own denominators. Revalidate risky/catch-all addresses using a documented freshness policy, not an invented safe status.

## Cadence and replies

Define channel order, timing/time zones, ownership, response SLA, maximum touches, and exit events before enrollment. A reply anywhere pauses other automated touches for the person. Stop on opt-out; propagate authorized suppression and verify its receipt. If no write authority is available, draft the suppression/handoff and request the needed action; do not claim an unsent request was executed.

Classify positive, information, objection, not-now, referral, out-of-office, opt-out, and bounce separately. Draft a direct factual response; a referral does not grant unrelated contact permissions. Propose real available meeting times in the recipient's time zone. Creating invites, reminders, CRM records, messages, or account configuration requires scoped authorization.

Use approved connectors where available. Keep LinkedIn manual/research workflows within current terms; do not assume a third-party automation tool is permitted. Define pause/escalation conditions from actual delivery, complaints, unresolved suppression, and capacity to answer. A successful API response alone is not a held meeting.

## Deliverable

Return a readiness checklist with evidence, cadence/exit rules, approved scope and caps, reply drafts, owner, measurement window, and missing permissions. Pilot a bounded eligible cohort before proposing expansion.

Current requirements: [Gmail sender guidelines](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/81126). Check Yahoo, Microsoft, and the selected sending provider separately rather than assuming parity.
