### Additional checks

- Use the available browser or authorized connected data source instead of making a particular vendor mandatory. Mark unavailable metrics honestly. Date observed facts, distinguish inference from implications, and do not infer absence, free pricing, motive, or unchanged history from one scrape.
- Use sourced buyer/deal evidence for claimed advantages; keep unsupported advantages as hypotheses. Route TAM/SAM/SOM work to `marketing-plan` with the scope and source evidence.

### Research boundary

Use `prospecting` for an account the user wants to sell to. A competitor dossier and a prospect brief serve different decisions; do not convert competitive research into contact enrollment or outreach authorization.

### Earned-link handoff

For turning evidenced competitor referring pages/domains into a page-level earned-link plan, use `link-building`. Do not interpret a proprietary authority score or raw link-count gap as proof of the ranking cause.
