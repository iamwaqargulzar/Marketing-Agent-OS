# Sponsorship and Exhibiting

Use this reference to evaluate, negotiate, execute, and measure paid event access.

## Go/no-go model

Estimate:

1. Reachable ICP attendees, not total attendance.
2. Realistic qualified interactions based on direct history or an explicitly labeled proxy.
3. All-in cost: fee, travel, lodging, production, logistics, staff time, and opportunity cost.
4. Cost per qualified conversation, meeting, and opportunity.
5. Counterfactual return from the strongest alternative channel.
6. Strategic value such as stage access, customer concentration, or category presence.

Ask organizers for evidence behind audience claims. Treat prospectus impressions, attendee counts, and lead promises as unverified until supported.

## Package design and negotiation

Prioritize useful access over logo inventory:

- relevant speaking or facilitated-session access;
- permission and space for an owned side event;
- documented attendee-data and consent mechanics;
- opt-in session registrants or legitimate lead capture;
- placement, timing, meeting space, and operational support;
- cancellation, recording, content, and data terms;
- price after the package supports the objective.

Do not purchase or sign terms without explicit authorization and appropriate legal/procurement review.

## Side-event option

A curated dinner, breakfast, or workshop may produce deeper interaction than a large booth. Model it independently: target audience, acceptance assumptions, venue and accessibility, host capacity, consent, total cost, follow-up, and expected qualified outcomes.

## Floor execution

- Staff with people able to qualify, explain, and record permitted context.
- Use a short relevance question rather than a generic pitch.
- Capture the problem, stated interest, consent state, and agreed next step—not sensitive personal commentary.
- Make non-ICP exits respectful and avoid manipulative giveaways or scans.
- Treat pre-booked meetings as a primary motion where appropriate.

## Measurement

Track qualified conversations, meetings, opportunities, influenced pipeline, and closed outcomes with a declared window. Keep booth traffic and scans as operational activity metrics. Compare cost per qualified outcome with alternatives and label attribution uncertainty.
