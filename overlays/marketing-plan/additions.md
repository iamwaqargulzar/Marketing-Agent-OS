### Budget and cohort reconciliation

Use matching closed windows for stage rates; label immature trials/signups pending. Bridge ending ARR from retained starting-base revenue plus retained new-cohort revenue, distinguishing revenue retention from logo retention and exposure time for new acquisitions. Required acquisitions are the nonnegative remaining ARR gap divided by retained ARR per acquisition; round customer count upward and multiply by evidenced CAC. Do not omit churn replacement, double-count expansion, or treat year-end ARR as cash receipts.
