# Earned-link planning and receipts

Prioritize the user's pages and audience over a domain-score target. For each tactic, record the relevant page, factual reason to include the asset, contact provenance, cost/material connection, permission, owner, and validation.

- **Reclamation:** verify a real unlinked mention or broken destination, then propose a relevant link/correction. Do not assume every mention owes a backlink.
- **Partners/integrations:** use a genuine relationship and helpful product evidence. Do not invent a partnership or construct a network solely to exchange ranking credit.
- **Resource or comparison pages:** show why the resource improves the editor's actual audience task; request honest consideration, not a guaranteed position or fabricated testimonial.
- **Original data/tools/templates:** document methods, rights, sample limitations, freshness, and useful output before pitching. A copied statistic collection does not become original research.
- **Expert/editorial contributions:** prioritize real relevance and editorial judgment. Route press/podcast/journalist opportunities to public-relations; avoid paid guest-post networks.

Before paying a vendor, inspect proposed sites/pages, placement terms, qualification/disclosure, evidence of a real audience, refund conditions, and any claimed relationship. Payment for a link intended to pass ranking credit is not an earned editorial link.

Verify the live destination anchor in HTML/rendered DOM, redirects, actual rel tokens, placement context, date, and accessibility. A missing qualifier on an inspected anchor means an unqualified link observed, not confirmed ranking credit; missing/uninspectable anchors remain missing/unverified. A mention without a link can be measured as a mention, not counted as a referring-domain placement.

Report funnel counts by target page with consistent windows. Referral visits, qualified conversions, rankings, and AI mentions have different denominators and confounders. If scheduling is requested, use the host's supported mechanism with approved state storage and bounded duplicate-safe outreach; otherwise return the plan only.

Primary policy checks: [Google link spam](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#link-spam) and [qualifying outbound links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/qualify-outbound-links). Recheck before execution; do not turn this reference into an implied outreach approval.
