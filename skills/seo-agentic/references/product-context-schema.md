# Product Marketing Context Schema

Store at `.agents/product-marketing.md` when the user requests persistence.

Required sections when known:

- Product overview and category
- Audience, ICP, segments and buying roles
- Jobs, pains, triggers and desired outcomes
- Alternatives and competitors
- Positioning, differentiation and reasons to believe
- Objections and risk
- Switching forces: push, pull, habit, anxiety
- Customer language / verbatims with provenance
- Brand voice and language constraints
- Offer, pricing/packaging facts and claims boundaries
- Proof points and evidence type
- Business goals, funnel metrics and constraints
- Markets, languages, compliance and channel constraints
- Known unknowns
- Last updated date and changelog

Never invent customer quotes, market share, performance, pricing, legal claims or product capabilities.
