# Quality Gates

Eight inherited discipline gates are normalized here:

- **TALE** — narrative truth, audience resonance, language coherence, evidence.
- **CORE-EEAT** — search/content quality, technical accessibility, usefulness, experience/expertise/authority/trust evidence.
- **CITE** — AI-answer citability: clarity, information gain, traceable facts, entity/source precision.
- **ECHO** — social native-fit, voice, participation, safety, measurement.
- **SEND** — email deliverability, consent, relevance, message quality, rendering/measurement.
- **ROAS** — paid-media economics, signal integrity, audience/creative fit, pacing and experiment quality.
- **STAR** — creator fit, authenticity, disclosure, rights, delivery and measurable impact.
- **RAMP** — launch readiness, alignment, messaging/assets, operations, monitoring and post-launch momentum.

A numerical score is never allowed to hide a critical veto.
