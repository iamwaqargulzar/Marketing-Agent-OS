# AI Visibility and Positioning

Use for entity accuracy, brand sentiment, third-party consensus, and AI-answer measurement.

1. Establish the supported product category, target buyer, differentiators, and approved claims. Compare the About page, documentation, product listings, and independent coverage for contradictions. Recommend corrections to owned profiles; do not fabricate endorsements, awards, review sites, or consensus.
2. Track mention, citation, factual accuracy, recommendation, and sentiment separately. A cited page can criticize the brand. A recommendation may lack a citation. Preserve the answer, cited URL, prompt, platform, and collection date.
3. Choose prompts from actual buying questions. Group wording variants, retain branded/non-branded and segment differences, and repeat independent runs. Report denominators and uncertainty. Treat third-party studies as dated proxy evidence rather than universal ranking rules.
4. For a rebrand or repositioning, baseline old and new category prompts and inventory inconsistent public profiles. Draft a coordinated update plan and monitor both segments; do not promise immediate model-memory changes.
5. Publish primary evidence that serves a real buyer: methods, product facts, documentation, useful comparisons, original research, and segment-specific answers. Select a page format for its intent and evidence. Self-ranked listicles and scaled category pages require the same truth and usefulness checks as other content.
6. Evaluate LinkedIn articles, feed posts, company pages, YouTube, or other third-party formats separately by target engine and period. Do not infer citations from engagement, follower counts, or one cited answer. Record crawling/indexing limitations, ownership, and update control before recommending dependence on a platform.
7. Triangulate prompt monitoring with referral/conversion data and optional self-reported attribution. A visibility score is not revenue attribution. When feedback identifies inaccurate claims, propose factual corrections and record which surfaces can actually be updated.

Use `format-volatility.md` for repeated-run measurement and technical/comprehension/selection diagnosis. Treat interview anecdotes as practitioner hypotheses until the user's evidence supports them.
