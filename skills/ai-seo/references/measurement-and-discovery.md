# AI answer evidence and agent discovery

## Measure the actual decision

Choose the business question and realistic buyer prompts before buying a tracker. Build a stable core cohort, a small experimental cohort, and a separately labeled campaign cohort from buyer language, sales questions, site queries, and known gaps. Record exact wording, engine/surface, model/search mode when observable, locale, capture time, and source access.

Repeat runs and distinguish within-session follow-ups from clean-session comparisons. Test multi-turn journeys as journeys; a follow-up depends on preceding messages. When prompts, models, languages, or markets change, version the panel and preserve the break rather than presenting unlike samples as one trend.

Measure presence, factual accuracy, recommendations, citations, and completion of the user's task separately. For an actionable claim, store the captured claim, current authoritative evidence, factual verdict/uncertainty, likely retrieved source (or unknown), business severity, owner, and retest condition. An unwanted opinion is not automatically a false fact.

Fix supported errors in the appropriate owned source; request factual third-party corrections as drafts, without pressure to remove fair criticism. Do not promise that a source edit will change model output. Missing sources and unrepeatable claims remain unknown.

## Impact beyond referrals

Separate answer exposure, immediate referral behavior, later branded/direct behavior, and deduplicated business outcomes. Missing referrers can hide exposure, but do not reassign all direct or brand traffic to AI. State windows, consent, coverage, confounders, and competing campaigns.

A correlation, pre/post movement, or simple comparison is not automatically causal. Prefer a valid randomized or otherwise defensible evaluation where feasible; report its assumptions, uncertainty, and interference limits. Do not import a vendor study's recovery/lift percentages as a client forecast.

## Agent task choice

For developer/API products, sample realistic unbranded tasks across relevant stacks in clean isolated environments. Record the product selected, retrieved docs, factual errors, authorization boundaries, and whether the task reached a working result. Selecting a vendor is not successful integration.

Default to non-mutating fixtures. Running a task panel does not authorize signups, paid provisioning, credential creation, deployment, or sending. Stop at those boundaries unless explicitly authorized. Compare task-shaped docs, version/prerequisite clarity, current examples, troubleshooting, pricing units, and consistent facts; do not instruct agents to override a user's vendor preference.

## Markets, commerce, and source mix

Run market/language cohorts with native buyer phrasing and relevant local competitors; translation alone is not a measurement panel. Date prices, availability, specifications, and source eligibility. For shopping surfaces, first verify that the product/category/market fits the actual consumer's current requirements and that permissioned feeds match visible product facts.

Classify retrieved sources by owned, editorial, review, community, reference, or other actual type. Choose outreach versus owned content from the observed mix, not a universal third-party dominance rule. Structured data and discovery files can support a documented consumer; neither is a guaranteed AI ranking or citation lever.

Return the panel version, captured evidence, claim/source issues, measured counts with denominators, business-outcome limits, prioritized fixes, and safe retest plan. Persist answers or business context only in an approved location.
