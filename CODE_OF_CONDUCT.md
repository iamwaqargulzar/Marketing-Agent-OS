# Code of conduct

Marketing Agent OS welcomes constructive participation from people of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Share reproducible evidence and disclose uncertainty.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licenses, and platform rules.
- Accept maintainer decisions gracefully, even when you disagree.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberately unsafe code, concealed credentials, spam, or deceptive automation.
- Manipulating community channels, issue trackers, rankings, reviews, or engagement.
- Repeated disruption after a maintainer asks for a behavior to stop.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates these expectations.

Use GitHub's private content-reporting and blocking tools for harassment or abuse. Do not post sensitive personal details publicly. Security vulnerabilities follow the separate private process in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

Enforcement decisions will prioritize safety, context, proportionality, and the long-term health of the project.
