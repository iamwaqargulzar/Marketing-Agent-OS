# Reviews and affiliate operations

## Honest review programs

Choose request-program, response, triage, or testimonial-reuse mode. Apply neutral experience-based eligibility and timing across positive, neutral, and negative feedback; do not gate public invitations using NPS/CSAT. Verify platform-specific invitation, incentive, automation, moderation, and reuse rules before proposing execution. Default to no incentive; disclosure cannot legalize a prohibited one.

Customers write their own reviews. Do not invent ratings, author customer reviews, suppress criticism, or condition compensation/remediation on praise or removal. Deduplicate invitations and honor opt-outs within the approved channel scope.

For a response, separate reviewer assertions from confirmed records. Draft a specific acknowledgment and actual next step without inventing an investigation, refund, or resolution. Keep account/payment/private details off public replies. Flag suspected violations only with evidence and permission; negative sentiment alone is not a violation.

For reuse, preserve source/date, exact relevant text, scope of permission, allowed edits/channels, attribution, material-connection disclosure, and withdrawal/expiry. A public review is not blanket advertising permission or substantiation for an unsupported performance claim. Label a curated positive set as selected testimonials, not all reviews.

Measure delivered unique invitations, published reviews with cohort/window, response time, complaint themes, and evidenced resolution. Invitations sent, replies published, and customer problems resolved are different facts.

## Affiliate operations

Specify partner fit, recruitment/activation responsibilities, tracking and attribution windows, eligible conversions, commission basis, refunds, fraud, payment timing, currency, disputes, disclosures, and termination before rollout. Reconcile payouts to the deduplicated transaction ledger and actual agreed terms, not a partner dashboard total alone.

Compare mature partner cohorts and contribution after payouts/serving cost; distinguish attributed sales from incremental growth and existing demand. Pilot a bounded partner group with reconciled receipts. Payments, enrollment, new terms, invitations, and publication require explicit scope; never implement an assumed standard commission.
