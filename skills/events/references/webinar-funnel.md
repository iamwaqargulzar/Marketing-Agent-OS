# Webinar Funnel

Use this reference for a hosted webinar from topic selection through follow-up.

## Define the funnel

Track:

`eligible visitor → registrant → attendee → engaged attendee → agreed next step → qualified outcome`

Use the organization's own history as the baseline. External conversion ranges are **estimated proxies**, not targets.

## Topic and offer

- Choose a problem-aware topic for one audience.
- State one concrete transformation or decision the session enables.
- Decide the legitimate next step before building content.
- Avoid product-only topics unless the event is explicitly for customers or users.
- Validate all urgency and scarcity claims.

## Registration and promotion

- Match the page headline to the event promise.
- Explain who it is for, what attendees can do afterward, speaker relevance, date/time/time zone, replay policy, and accessibility details.
- Collect only fields needed for delivery and qualification.
- Define consent separately for organizers, partners, SMS, recording, and subsequent marketing.
- Use trackable but privacy-respecting promotion across owned, partner, social, and paid channels.

## Attendance

- Provide an immediate confirmation and usable calendar entry.
- Plan a bounded reminder sequence based on consent and audience expectations.
- Put the join link where attendees can retrieve it safely.
- Use optional pre-event questions only when their collection and retention are justified.
- Measure calendar-add and reminder performance before assuming the topic failed.

## Live session

Structure the session around promise, useful teaching, evidence, a transparent transition, one next step, and enough time for questions. Avoid disguised sales pitches and false urgency. Design for captions, keyboard access, readable visuals, and post-event transcript quality.

## Follow-up

Segment by observed engagement and consent:

| Segment | Appropriate next step |
|---|---|
| Engaged attendee | Contextual follow-up and agreed action |
| Early departure | Replay pointer to relevant material and a softer next step |
| No-show | Replay or summary if permitted; avoid assuming sales intent |
| Replay-only registrant | Deliver the promised asset under the stated consent terms |

Reuse recordings, transcripts, clips, and claims only when rights are documented. Record source, campaign, and attribution assumptions before interpreting downstream pipeline.

## Diagnose by stage

- Low registration: inspect audience, topic, promise, page, and traffic quality.
- Low attendance: inspect time zone, calendar delivery, reminders, lead time, and audience source.
- Low engagement: inspect expectation match, pacing, accessibility, and content value.
- High engagement but low next-step conversion: inspect offer fit, transition clarity, and friction.
- Weak pipeline: inspect qualification, follow-up, attribution, and whether the event should have had a pipeline objective at all.
